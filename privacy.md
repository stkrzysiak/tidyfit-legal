# Tidy Fit Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 23, 2026
**Contact:** stkrzysiak@gmail.com

Tidy Fit ("the app", "we") is a fitness app that turns household chores into guided
workout routines. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## The short version

- Tidy Fit does not require an account and does not collect personal data.
- Your workout progress (points, streaks, completed sessions, badges) is stored
  **only on your device**.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, track you across apps, or share data with
  data brokers.

## Data stored on your device

- Completed sessions, points, streak, and earned badges
- App preferences (for example, the selected music track)

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

## Data we collect

None. The current version of Tidy Fit makes no network requests for user data and
has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs enabled.

## What we do NOT collect

- No account, email, or name
- No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no tracking across other apps
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, or microphone access
- No Apple Health, HealthKit, Google Fit, or Health Connect data

## Health note

Workout activity in Tidy Fit is self-reported chore and exercise activity that you
log by completing routines. It is not medical data and is not shared with anyone.

## Future features

If a later version adds optional features that send data off your device (for
example, account sync or a premium subscription), we will update this policy, bump
the effective date, and describe the change in the release notes before those
features are enabled.

## Children

Tidy Fit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and note
the change in the app's release notes.

## Contact

Questions or data requests: stkrzysiak@gmail.com
